About Me

Strategy meets storytelling

How I think

Evidence first, then the human insight.

Every idea starts with evidence: search demand, engagement signals, audience behaviour and conversion potential. Human insight is what makes people care. I work where the two meet.

Behind the work

Finding the gap between perception and experience.

I look for the gap between how a brand wants to be seen and how it is actually experienced. That gap can sit in the message, the follow-up, the process or the offer positioning.

I turn what I observe into audits, campaign ideas, follow-up flows, process documents or a clearer direction.

My Approach

Four steps, every brief

I start with what the customer actually sees and feels, then turn the gaps into something a team can act on.

See it in action
01

Observe

The brand, audience, offer, communication and the visible customer journey.

02

Identify

Anything unclear, inconsistent, delayed, missing or hard to trust.

03

Structure

Audits, workflows, scripts, SOPs, campaigns or execution plans.

04

Execute

Practical output that helps a business look clearer, follow up better and operate with less confusion.
